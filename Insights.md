# Key Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Regional Analysis

The West region demonstrated the strongest sales and profitability performance, indicating a highly responsive market.

## 2. Category Performance

Technology products generated the highest profit margins, while Furniture showed weaker profitability due to aggressive discounting.

## 3. Customer Segmentation

The Consumer segment contributed the highest percentage of total sales, making it the most valuable customer group.

## 4. Discount Strategy

A strong negative relationship was observed between discount rates and profit margins.

## 5. Growth Trends

Monthly sales trends indicate overall growth with seasonal fluctuations across regions.
