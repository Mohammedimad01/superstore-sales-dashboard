# 🚀 Project Overview

An interactive **Retail Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard** built using **Power BI** and the popular **Superstore Sales Dataset** from Kaggle.

The dashboard provides deep business insights into:

* Sales performance
* Profitability trends
* Regional performance
* Product analysis
* Customer segment contribution
* Discount impact on profit

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## ✅ Regional Performance

* West region generated the highest sales and profit.
* East region also showed strong market performance.
* Central region underperformed compared to other regions.

## ✅ Product & Category Insights

* Technology category generated the highest profit margins.
* Furniture category had lower profitability despite high discounts.
* Certain products significantly outperformed others in revenue contribution.

## ✅ Customer Segment Analysis

* Consumer segment generated the largest share of sales.
* Corporate and Home Office segments showed stable contribution patterns.

## ✅ Discount Impact

* Higher discounts negatively impacted profit margins.
* Furniture products were heavily affected by discounting strategies.
